About WebIntel AI

AI-Powered Web Scraper & Intelligent Data Analytics Platform - Department of CSE, IET Lucknow, 2026-27

Problem

Useful information is spread across many sites. Manual collection is slow and inconsistent, and basic scrapers collect text without explaining it. WebIntel AI joins extraction, cleaning, AI analysis, history and export in one workspace.

Technology

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend (full version): Python, FastAPI
  • Scraping: Requests, BeautifulSoup
  • Data: SQLite, Pandas

Security & responsible scraping

  • SSRF protection (backend version)
  • robots.txt respected (backend version)
  • Treat scraped content as untrusted
  • No bypassing logins, CAPTCHAs or paywalls
